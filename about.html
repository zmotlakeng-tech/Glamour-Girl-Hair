Our Story

Glamour Girl Hair was founded in 2022 with one goal: to bring premium Vietnamese straight, super double-drawn hair bundles to South African women who want a luxury look without the luxury markup.

Glamour Girl Hair is a small business which was founded in 2022 that sells a variety of bundles from Vietnamese too Brazilian bundles. The texture of the hairs differs, as we have the straight bundles together with the water wave bundles.

Our Mission & Vision

Glamour Girl Hair's vision is to expand globally and be the most trusted and glamorous hair brand that empowers women to express their beauty, confidence, and individuality through premium quality hair.

What Empowers Us

Glamour Girl Hair uses in a high-demand beauty market with strong brand positioning. It's key strength includes a memorable brand that conveys luxury, a product that is visually marketable on social media, and multiple revenue streams through product sales and services.
